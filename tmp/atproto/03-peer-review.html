Strong methodological paper comparing single vs. multi-marker eDNA approaches. The 40% increase in taxon recovery (COI+ITS1+ITS2 vs COI alone) across 8 sites is well-documented with 702 ASVs. The marker complementarity analysis showing minimal overlap between fungal (ITS1/ITS2) and metazoan (COI) markers is particularly valuable. Bioinformatic pipeline is well-documented and reproducible. Main weakness is the presentation — figures could be more informative.
